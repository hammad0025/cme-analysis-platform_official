STANDARD OF CARE REFERENCES

Range of Motion Measurement

Per AMA Guides to the Evaluation of Permanent Impairment and AAOS standards, objective ROM assessment requires:

  • Use of calibrated goniometer or inclinometer
  • Documentation of specific measurements in degrees
  • Comparison to established normal values
  • Multiple measurements (3x) for reliability

Normal Cervical ROM (AMA Guides): Flexion 50°, Extension 60°, Lateral Flexion 45° each side, Rotation 80° each side

Normal Shoulder ROM: Forward Flexion 180°, Abduction 180°, External Rotation 90°, Internal Rotation 90°

Citation: Hirsch et al. (2014) demonstrated that visual ROM estimation is NOT accurate. Am J Orthop, 43(11), E261-E265.

Neurological Examination

Complete neurological examination should include:

  • Motor strength testing (graded 0-5) - upper AND lower extremities
  • Deep tendon reflexes (graded 0-4+) - biceps, triceps, brachioradialis, patellar, Achilles
  • Sensory testing (light touch, pinprick, proprioception)
  • Cranial nerve assessment (all 12 nerves for completeness)
  • Coordination and gait assessment
  • Special tests: Hoffman's sign, Babinski reflex for upper motor neuron lesions
